ICSC2026 Tupipa Camera-Ready Supplementary Listening Report

Purpose
-------
This report documents the informal qualitative listening check used during
final calibration of the four-layer Csound Tupipa model. It is supplied for
transparency and reproducibility. It is not a formal perceptual experiment.

Participants
------------
The available record identifies the following participant backgrounds:

- The author.
- Two conservatory professors with expertise in Chinese traditional music and
  sound design.
- Experienced local Tupipa performers and cultural practitioners.

The exact total number of listeners was not retained in the available record.
Individual names, ages, hearing-screening information, session date, and
playback hardware were not recorded.

Materials compared
------------------
The group compared the target recording, the complete four-layer model, and
isolated layer renders. The supplementary package contains the corresponding
stereo WAV examples:

- 01_complete_model_stereo.wav
- 02_core_string_stereo.wav
- 03_body_response_stereo.wav
- 04_plectrum_contact_stereo.wav
- 05_friction_noise_stereo.wav

The discussion focused on attack articulation, brightness, body coloration,
friction/noise prominence, and sustain/decay. The comparison was made after
the acoustic-descriptor diagnostics and bounded parameter sweeps had narrowed
the candidate settings.

Qualitative feedback retained
-----------------------------
The retained oral feedback favored:

- A moderate attack rather than an excessively bright or sharp attack.
- A distinct but subordinate plectrum-contact transient.
- Restrained friction/noise energy that remains audible as texture without
  masking the pitched core.
- A longer body-supported decay rather than an abruptly disappearing tail.

Calibration decisions
---------------------
The final calibration retained the four-layer organization and used the
following confirmed settings in the complete source file: iCps=369.0 Hz,
kamp=0.30, iPluckPos=0.220, kpick=0.400, krefl=0.624, core gain=0.29,
body-response gain=0.78, plectrum-contact gain=0.005, and friction/noise gain
=0.11. These choices were checked together with the acoustic diagnostics and
were not selected from listening feedback alone.

Limitations of this report
--------------------------
No per-listener scores, pairwise preference counts, inter-rater agreement,
session date, playback hardware, or controlled listening-room description was
retained. Accordingly, this report makes no statistical perceptual claim and
should be read as a qualitative calibration record only. The evidence concerns
one Tupipa, one open-string register, and isolated plucks.
